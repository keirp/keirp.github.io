- I am fortunate to be advised by [Jimmy Ba](https://jimmylba.github.io/) and [Sheila McIlraith](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~sheila/).
- I am also affiliated with the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/).
